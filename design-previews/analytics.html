Why turnovers cost 14.4

Indiana gave the ball away 23 times to Oklahoma City’s 8. Each extra turnover is allocated one lost shot opportunity.

(23 − 8) × 0.9584 = 14.415 opportunities × average points per FGA
Accounting assumptions

This is an opportunity estimate. Turnovers and offensive rebounds allocate shot attempts; possession balance reconciles the remaining difference. Shooting accuracy, shot mix and free throws are separated so each contribution is counted once. The model explains the margin; it does not predict an alternate result.
